03

Slides

Move the amber ring onto PREV or NEXT and pinch to press. Or point-and-hold at a camera edge, or swipe an open palm.

1 / 5
  1. Pinch-press the big PREV / NEXT buttons with the air cursor.
  2. Point & hold your index finger at the right edge of the camera frame for 0.6 s → next. Left edge → back.
  3. Palm swipe: open hand, sweep it sideways quickly (in about a third of a second) → next or back. The bar shows how fast the swipe is.
  4. After a change there is a short pause (0.9 s) so one move is not counted twice.

05

OS Control

Run python -m bridge and open this page from http://localhost:8000. Push the lever up to arm (it links to the bridge by itself). Point moves the real cursor; a quick pinch clicks; a pinch held over 450 ms drags; a peace sign moved up or down scrolls.

  1. Stop: KILL, Esc, a fist held 1 s, hand out of view, switching mode or window, or an open palm held 2 s. The lever drops and nothing re-arms by itself.
  2. F9 (or Esc) in any window stops the real bridge even when the browser is not focused.
  3. The bridge listens on localhost only, checks the page origin, rate-limits input and releases a held button after 1.5 s without input.
