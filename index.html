    Mode 06

    OS Control

    Run python -m bridge, then connect. Control starts paused; tick Enable OS control. Point moves the real cursor; a quick pinch clicks where the cursor was 200 ms earlier; a pinch held over 450 ms drags; a peace sign moved up or down scrolls. Esc, a fist held 1 s, or losing the hand releases the mouse and pauses. An open palm held 2 s pauses or re-enables. If the bridge pauses itself (watchdog, kill, fail-safe), tick the toggle again.

    Action: idlePalm hold

    The bridge listens on localhost only, checks the page origin, rate-limits input and pauses itself after 1.5 s without a ping.
